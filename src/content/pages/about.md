      SISI Clinic is a small, private studio in St James Tower on Charlotte Street, four minutes' walk from Piccadilly station. It is a quiet room, not a busy salon floor.

      Sugaring is at the heart of what we do. We use a warm paste of sugar, water and lemon, applied at around body temperature, and we take the time to do it carefully. Laser hair removal is also available, for long-term hair reduction over a course of sessions.

      Appointments are calm and unhurried. We explain each step before we start, so you always know what is happening, and you set the pace.

      Women and men are welcome, whether it is your first visit or you have been sugaring for years. We speak English, Romanian and Russian.
