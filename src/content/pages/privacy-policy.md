## What we collect on this website

### The contact form

When you send a question through our [contact form](/contact/), we receive your name, your email address, your phone number if you choose to give it, and your message. The form sends these to our email inbox.

We use them only to reply to you. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in answering the questions people send us, or taking steps you ask for before you book with us. We keep form messages for up to 12 months after our last reply, then delete them.

Please don't send health details through the form. We will ask about your skin and health at your consultation instead.

To stop automated spam, the form uses a scrambled (hashed) code made from your IP address to count how many messages are sent from the same connection in an hour. It is not linked to your message and is used for nothing else.

### Phone, WhatsApp and email

If you call, message or email us, we use your details and messages to answer you and to manage your appointments. We keep these messages in the same way as contact-form messages, unless they become part of your client record (see below). WhatsApp is run by WhatsApp (part of Meta), which has its own privacy policy.

### Technical information

Like any website, our hosting provider's servers may record basic technical information, such as your IP address, the type of browser you use and the pages you request. This is used to deliver the website and keep it secure. We do not use it to identify you or to follow what you do.

## Cookies

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking tools, so there is no cookie banner.

Some pages have a Treatwell booking panel. Nothing loads from Treatwell until you choose to show the panel or press a Book button. If you do, you are using Treatwell's booking system, and Treatwell's own cookies and privacy notice apply.

## Booking through Treatwell

Online bookings are made through Treatwell. Treatwell acts as its own controller for the information you give it when you book, such as your account, contact and payment details, and handles it under [Treatwell's privacy notice](https://www.treatwell.co.uk/info/privacy-policy/). Payments you make online are processed by Treatwell.

Treatwell passes us the details we need for your appointment, such as your name, contact details, the treatment and the time. From then on, we hold them as part of your client record.

## Information we hold when you are our client

When you book or visit us, we keep:

- your contact details: name, phone number and email address
- your appointment history: the treatments you had, when, and what you paid
- your consultation and patch-test answers about your skin and health, for example medication, pregnancy and skin conditions, and notes about your treatments

We use this information to book and provide your treatments, to keep you safe and to meet the requirements of our insurance. Our lawful basis is the contract between us for your treatments, and our legitimate interest in keeping accurate treatment records.

Information about your health is special category data. We process it only with your explicit consent, which we ask for at your consultation. You can withdraw your consent at any time. If you do, we will not be able to carry out treatments that depend on it. We may still keep the records we already hold for as long as we need them to deal with any claim.

We keep your client record for treatment safety and insurance for up to 7 years after your last treatment. After that, we delete it or destroy it securely.

## Who we share information with

We do not sell your information. These companies process data for us, under our instructions:

- **GoDaddy**, which hosts this website and runs the contact form
- **Microsoft 365**, which we use for email

Some of these providers may store or access data outside the UK. Where they do, they use safeguards approved under UK data protection law.

Treatwell handles your booking as its own controller, as explained above. We may also share information when the law requires it, or with our insurer or legal advisers if we need to deal with a claim.

We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure and to limit access to the people who need it.

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

- **access** the personal information we hold about you
- have inaccurate information **corrected**
- have your information **erased** in some circumstances
- **restrict** how we use your information in some circumstances
- **object** to how we use your information, where we rely on legitimate interests
- **data portability**: receive the information you gave us in a format you can reuse
- **withdraw your consent** at any time, where we rely on it

To use any of these rights, email [contact@sisiclinic.co.uk](mailto:contact@sisiclinic.co.uk). We may ask you to confirm your identity. There is usually no charge. We will reply within one month. If your request is complex, we may need up to two more months, and we will tell you if so.

## Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first at [contact@sisiclinic.co.uk](mailto:contact@sisiclinic.co.uk). We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and let you know what we are doing about it.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK regulator for data protection: [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk), helpline 0303 123 1113.
