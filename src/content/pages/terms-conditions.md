## Changing or cancelling

To change or cancel an online booking, use the link in your confirmation email, within the cancellation window shown when you book. You can also contact us by phone, WhatsApp or email.

Late cancellations and missed appointments are handled under the policy shown on the booking page when you book.

If we need to cancel or move your appointment, we will tell you as soon as we can and offer you another time. If you paid in advance and do not want another time, we will arrange a full refund of what you paid.

## Arriving on time

Please arrive on time, ideally a few minutes early, so we have time for a short chat before we start. If you are late, we may need to shorten your treatment so the next client is not kept waiting, or rebook it for another time.

## Your health and suitability

Before your first treatment, we ask about your skin, health and medication. Please tell us, before every treatment, about:

- any medication, including retinoids and acne medication
- pregnancy
- any skin condition, and any changes since your last visit

We may decline or postpone a treatment if it is not safe or suitable for you. If we do, we will explain why and help you rebook when it is suitable.

For **laser** (not IPL):

- laser hair removal is for clients aged 18 and over only
- a free consultation and patch test is needed at least 24 hours before your first session
- most people need a course of 6 to 10 sessions, 4 to 6 weeks apart

Please follow the [aftercare advice](/aftercare/) we give you. If you have any reaction after a treatment, contact us straight away, and seek medical advice if you need it.

## Results

Results vary from person to person, so we cannot promise a particular result.

- **Sugaring** removes hair from the root. Results usually last three to five weeks, depending on how fast your hair grows. It can sting for a moment, especially on a first visit.
- **Laser** gives long-term hair reduction over a course of sessions. Results vary by skin tone and hair colour.

We will always carry out your treatment with reasonable care and skill.
