  - type: steps
    eyebrow: Before you come
    title: Four things to do beforehand
    items:
      - title: Let it grow
        text: Hair should be about 5 mm long, the length of a grain of rice. That is roughly two weeks after shaving. If it is longer, we can trim it.
      - title: Exfoliate two days before
        text: Gently exfoliate the area two days before your appointment, so the paste can reach the hair easily.
      - title: No lotion on the day
        text: Skip body lotion and oil on the area on the day. Clean, dry skin helps the paste grip the hair.
      - title: Wear something loose
        text: Loose, comfortable clothing is kinder to freshly treated skin, especially after bikini or leg sugaring.
  - type: text
    eyebrow: Worth telling us
    title: A few things to mention before we start
    body: |
      - **Your period.** Sugaring is fine with a tampon or a menstrual cup. Your skin can be a little more sensitive around your period, so some clients prefer to book a few days later.
      - **Medication and skincare.** Tell us if you use retinoids, acne medication or prescription skin creams. They can make skin more sensitive, and we may advise waiting.
      - **Skin conditions.** Let us know about eczema, psoriasis, sunburn, cuts or anything else on the area you would like treated.
      - **Pregnancy.** Tell us if you are pregnant. We will advise you, and some treatments may need to wait.
      - **Booking laser?** Laser (not IPL) starts with a free consultation and patch test at least 24 hours before your first session. It is for clients aged 18 and over, and most people need a course of 6 to 10 sessions, 4 to 6 weeks apart. Read more about [laser hair removal](/laser-hair-removal/).

  - type: imageText
    eyebrow: The treatment
    title: How it works and how it feels
    body: |
      The skin is cleansed and dusted lightly. Your therapist then works a small amount of warm paste, at around body temperature, against the direction of hair growth, and flicks it off in the direction the hair grows. This takes the hair out from the root, one small section at a time.

      To be honest, it can sting for a moment with each flick, more so on a first visit and on intimate areas. Many clients tell us it was quicker and more bearable than they expected, and it usually gets easier with regular visits. Tell us whenever you would like a pause.

      Small areas such as the upper lip or underarms take around 20 to 25 minutes. A Hollywood or Brazilian takes around 35 to 45 minutes, and full legs about an hour. We finish by checking for stray hairs and calming the skin.

  - type: text
    eyebrow: Afterwards
    title: Looking after your skin
    body: |
      A little redness is normal and usually settles within a few hours. For the next day or two:

      - Avoid hot baths, saunas, pools, the gym and tanning for 48 hours.
      - Don't use deodorant on your underarms for 24 hours.
      - Wear loose cotton clothing.
      - Use SPF on treated skin in the sun.
      - Start gentle exfoliation after a few days, which may help reduce ingrown hairs.

      Results usually last three to five weeks, and most clients rebook every four to six weeks. Read the full [aftercare guide](/aftercare/) for more.

    items:
      - title: I feel embarrassed
        text: That's very common, and there is no need. Nothing about your body will surprise us. We talk you through each step, and you set the pace.
      - title: Will it hurt?
        text: It can sting for a moment, especially the first time. The paste is warm rather than hot, and most clients find it more manageable than they expected.
      - title: Will my skin be red?
        text: Some redness or small bumps are normal and usually settle within a few hours to a day. If you have a special event, book your first visit a few days before it.
      - title: Will the hair grow back thicker?
        text: No. Removing hair from the root doesn't make it grow back thicker. With regular visits, many clients find their hair grows back finer.
      - title: I'm a man. Is that okay?
        text: Of course. We sugar backs, chests, shoulders, arms, legs and more. Our men's menu doesn't include intimate areas. See [men's sugaring](/sugaring-hair-removal/men/).

        text: Yes. Tell us at any point and we will pause. You are in charge of the pace.
