    title: Sugar, water and lemon. That's the paste.
    body: |
      Sugaring is one of the oldest ways to remove hair. The paste is made from sugar, water and lemon, warmed to around body temperature and worked into the skin against the direction of hair growth. It is then flicked off in the direction the hair grows, taking the hair out from the root.

      Because the paste grips the hair rather than the skin, many people with sensitive skin find it more comfortable than waxing. There is no hot wax and no strips.
