We want the reviews on this website to give you a fair and honest picture of what it is like to visit SISI Clinic. This page explains where they come from and how we handle them.

## Where the reviews on this site come from

The reviews you see on this website come from two places:

- **Google reviews.** Reviews left on our Google Business Profile, copied exactly as the client wrote them, with the name and date shown on Google.
- **Reviews sent to us directly.** Feedback that clients send us by email or message, which we publish only with the client's permission. These are marked as "Direct".

Each review shows its source and the date it was written.

## How we choose which reviews to show

We add the newest reviews to the website regularly, whatever the rating. We show the most recent ones first. We do not pick out only the best reviews, and we do not hide negative ones.

## What we never do

- We never edit a review. What you read is what the client wrote.
- We never pay for reviews, or offer discounts, free treatments or anything else in return for a review.
- We never write reviews ourselves, and we never ask our staff to write or post reviews, or give anyone set wording to use.
- We never filter reviews, or only ask the clients we think are happy to leave one.

## Treatwell reviews

Many of our clients book through Treatwell and leave reviews there. Those reviews stay on Treatwell, and we do not copy them onto this website. We show only our overall Treatwell rating and number of reviews, with the date we last checked them and a link to read the reviews on Treatwell.

## Reporting a review you think is fake

If you think a review on this website is fake or misleading, please email us at [contact@sisiclinic.co.uk](mailto:contact@sisiclinic.co.uk). Tell us the name and date shown on the review and why you are concerned. We will look into it, and if the review does not meet this policy, we will remove it.

You can also report a Google review directly to Google, or a Treatwell review directly to Treatwell.
