  - type: features
    eyebrow: After sugaring
    title: The first 24 to 48 hours
    items:
      - title: Keep it cool
        text: Avoid hot baths, saunas, steam rooms and swimming pools for 48 hours. A lukewarm shower is fine.
      - title: Rest from the gym
        text: Skip the gym and hard exercise for 48 hours. Heat and sweat can irritate freshly treated skin.
      - title: No tanning
        text: No sunbeds or fake tan for 48 hours. If you are out in the sun, use SPF on treated skin.
      - title: Underarms
        text: Don't use deodorant on your underarms for 24 hours.
      - title: Wear loose cotton
        text: Loose cotton clothing and underwear let the skin breathe, especially after bikini sugaring.
      - title: Face
        text: After facial sugaring, keep make-up off the area for 12 hours and avoid active skincare such as acids and retinoids for 48 hours.
  - type: text
    eyebrow: The days after
    title: Exfoliating, ingrown hairs and your next visit
    body: |
      **Start gently exfoliating a few days after your appointment.** Use a soft mitt or a mild scrub two or three times a week, and moisturise afterwards. This may help reduce ingrown hairs.

      **If you notice an ingrown hair,** try not to pick or squeeze it. Keep exfoliating gently and wear loose clothing. If it becomes sore, swollen or inflamed, speak to a pharmacist or your GP. If you are prone to ingrown hairs, tell us at your next visit and we will suggest what to do between appointments.

      **When to rebook.** Results usually last three to five weeks, depending on how fast your hair grows. Most clients rebook every four to six weeks, once the hair is about 5 mm long again. Try not to shave in between, so the hair is long enough for your next appointment.
  - type: features
    eyebrow: After laser
    title: Looking after your skin between laser sessions
    intro: Laser (not IPL) works over a course of sessions, so your aftercare matters every time.
    items:
      - title: SPF every day
        text: Use SPF on treated skin whenever it is exposed to daylight, and keep it covered in strong sun.
      - title: No tanning for two weeks
        text: Avoid sun, sunbeds and fake tan on the treated area for two weeks after each session.
      - title: Keep it cool for 48 hours
        text: No hot baths, saunas, steam rooms or pools for 48 hours after each session.
      - title: Shave only
        text: Between sessions you can shave the area if you need to, but don't sugar, wax or pluck, as the laser needs the hair root in place.
      - title: Warmth and redness
        text: The skin may feel warm and look a little red for a few hours. This usually settles on its own.
      - title: Stick to your plan
        text: Most people need 6 to 10 sessions, 4 to 6 weeks apart. Results vary by skin tone and hair colour, and build up session by session.
