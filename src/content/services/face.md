Facial skin is delicate, so we work in small sections with warm paste and a light touch. Sugaring removes the whole hair from the root, so regrowth looks softer than after shaving.

Tell us about any skincare you use, especially retinoids, acids or prescription creams. These can make the skin too sensitive for hair removal, and we will advise you if we need to wait.
