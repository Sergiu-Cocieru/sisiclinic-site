Laser hair removal targets the pigment in the hair to reduce regrowth over a course of sessions. It is not a one-off treatment: results build up session by session and vary from person to person, depending on your skin tone, hair colour and hormones.

This is **laser**, not IPL. Your first step is always the free consultation and patch test, where we check that laser is right for you and set up a personal plan. Laser hair removal is for clients aged 18 and over.
