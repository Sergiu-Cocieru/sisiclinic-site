It is the question almost everyone asks before their first appointment, so here is a straight answer. Yes, sugaring can sting for a moment. Each time the paste is flicked off, you feel a quick, sharp sensation, and then it fades.

Most of our clients tell us it was quicker and more bearable than they feared. Many people who have tried waxing find sugaring more comfortable. But everyone is different, and it helps to know what to expect.

## What it feels like

When the paste goes on, it feels warm, a little like warm honey spread over the skin. It is used at around body temperature, so it is not hot.

The sting comes when the paste is flicked off and the hair comes out. Most people describe it as a quick snap or pinch. It lasts about a second and fades quickly.

Afterwards your skin may feel warm and look a little pink. This usually calms down within a few hours.

## How it feels by area

Every body is different, but this is what most clients tell us.

- **Legs and arms.** Among the most comfortable areas to sugar. Many people find full legs easier than they expected.
- **Underarms.** The skin is thin and the hair is often dense, so it can sting a little more for a moment. It is over quickly.
- **Bikini, Hollywood and Brazilian.** The most sensitive area for most people, especially on a first visit. It usually gets easier with regular appointments.
- **Face.** The upper lip can make your eyes water for a second. The chin, cheeks and eyebrows are small areas and quick to do.
- **Back and chest.** For men with thick hair, the first session can feel stronger. It usually gets easier as the hair grows back finer.

## What makes it easier

A few simple things make a real difference.

**The right hair length.** About 5 mm, roughly the length of a grain of rice, is ideal. That is usually around two weeks after shaving. Hair that is much longer pulls more, and hair that is too short is harder for the paste to grip. Our guide on [how long hair should be for sugaring](/how-long-should-hair-be-for-sugaring/) explains more.

**Timing around your period.** Your skin can be more sensitive in the days just before and during your period. You can still come, with a tampon or a menstrual cup. But if you have the choice, book a few days later.

**Regular visits.** The first visit is usually the most noticeable. If you come back every four to six weeks, many people find the hair grows back finer and each visit feels easier.

**Prepared skin.** Exfoliate gently two days before your appointment. Skip body lotion and oil on the day, so the paste can grip the hair properly.

**Breathing.** It sounds simple, but it helps. Breathe in as the paste is smoothed on, and breathe out slowly as it is flicked off. Try not to hold your breath or tense up, as tense muscles make it feel stronger.

**Loose clothing.** Wear something comfortable, so nothing rubs your skin on the way home.

## Is there anything I should tell you first?

Yes. Please let us know if you use retinoids or acne medication, if you are pregnant, or if you have a skin condition. These can make your skin more sensitive, and in some cases we may suggest waiting or treating a smaller area first.

## After your appointment

For 48 hours, avoid hot baths, saunas, pools, the gym and tanning. If you had your underarms done, skip deodorant for 24 hours. Wear loose cotton, and start gentle exfoliation a few days later. Our [aftercare guide](/aftercare/) has the details.
