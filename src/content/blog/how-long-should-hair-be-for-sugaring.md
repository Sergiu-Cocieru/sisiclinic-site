Getting the length right is the simplest way to make your sugaring appointment go smoothly. The short answer is about 5 mm, roughly the length of a grain of rice.

Here is why it matters, how long to wait after shaving, and what to do if your hair is too long or too short.

## The ideal length is about 5 mm

Aim for hair about 5 mm long. If you are not sure, compare it with a grain of rice. It does not need to be exact.

At this length the paste has enough hair to hold, so each hair can come out whole, from the root. It also tends to feel more comfortable than sugaring very long hair.

## Why length matters

**Too short** and the paste cannot hold the hair properly. Some hairs may be left behind, and you may need to come back sooner.

**Too long** and the hair can tangle in the paste and pull more, which can feel less comfortable. It also takes longer to work through.

Around 5 mm gives the cleanest result and a more comfortable appointment.

## How long to wait after shaving

For most people, 5 mm is about two weeks after shaving. Hair grows at different speeds on different parts of the body, and from one person to the next, so use this as a guide:

- **Legs and arms.** Usually around two weeks.
- **Underarms.** Hair here often grows faster, so check it after about ten days.
- **Bikini area.** Around two weeks, sometimes a few days more.
- **Face.** The hair is finer, so check the length rather than counting days.

The easiest way is to look. When the hair is about as long as a grain of rice, you are ready to book.

## If you have waxed or used a cream

If you have waxed recently, the hair was removed from the root, so it takes longer to grow back. Wait until the regrowth reaches about 5 mm. That is often three to five weeks, the same as after sugaring.

Hair removal cream dissolves the hair at the surface, so treat it like shaving and wait about two weeks.

If you are having laser on an area, talk to us before you sugar it. Between laser sessions you should shave that area rather than sugar or wax it, because laser works on the hair in the follicle.

## What if my hair is too long?

Please do not worry. If it is much longer than 5 mm, we can trim it at the start of your appointment. You do not need to do anything.

If you would rather trim it yourself, use small scissors or a trimmer with a guard, and leave about 5 mm. Do not shave it off, or you will need to wait another two weeks.

## What if it is too short?

If your appointment is coming up and the hair still looks very short, it is often better to move the appointment a few days later. You can change or cancel using the link in your Treatwell confirmation email, or call or WhatsApp us on 07840 820525.

## Between appointments

Once you start sugaring, the aim is to let the hair grow without interruption.

- **Try not to shave.** Shaving cuts every hair at the surface and puts you back to the start. It can also make ingrown hairs more likely.
- **Avoid plucking.** Tweezers can snap hairs below the skin.
- **Rebook every four to six weeks.** Results usually last three to five weeks, depending on how fast your hair grows, and most clients book their next visit around then.
- **Exfoliate gently.** From a few days after your appointment, gentle exfoliation helps new hairs come through. Read more in our guide to [sugaring and ingrown hairs](/sugaring-hair-removal-ingrown-hairs/).

After a few regular appointments, many people find their hair grows back finer, so the weeks in between become easier.

## The day of your appointment

A few quick reminders:

- Hair about 5 mm long.
- Exfoliate gently two days before, not on the day.
- No body lotion or oil on the area on the day.
- Wear loose, comfortable clothing.

Laser is the opposite. For laser, you shave the area 24 hours before your session. We explain everything at your free consultation and patch test.
